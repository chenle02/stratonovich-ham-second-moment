\begin{remark}[Purpose of these calculations]\label{R:G1-scope}
  The static exponent from the published Chen--Hu theorem is
  $(4-\alpha)/(3-\alpha)$; see~\cite{chen.hu:25:hyperbolic}*{Theorem~1.2, (1.9),
  p.~2147}. Both $\kappa$ and the G0 exponent $\rho$ have this boundary limit.
  The finite coefficient limits above are justified separately by dominated
  convergence; they do not exchange $t \to \infty$ and $\alpha_0\downarrow0$ and
  do not verify an asymptotic constant. In particular, the disputed printed
  white-space constant in the separate Chen--Hu source audit is not used here.
  The single-chain rows agree with the first-moment expansion, and all mixed
  rows are nonnegative, consistent with the Jensen comparison. Relative to the
  Skorohod expansion, the rows with self-pairings are present here and cannot be
  dropped: already $M_2 = \E X_1^2+2\E X_2$. This is a structural comparison
  only, not a Skorohod result. Finally, any fixed truncation is a polynomial in
  $t^\beta$ with nonnegative coefficients, so its logarithm divided by
  $t^\kappa$ tends to zero. Orders two and four can falsify a normalization or
  an incorrect pairing rule, but cannot determine an all-order factorial power
  or prove either principal goal.
\end{remark}
