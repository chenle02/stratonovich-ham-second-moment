    Apply Proposition 34.4 with c = 0, reference Wm , horizon B and the deterministic
direction ϕm . Equality of drivers before ℓm and Young uniqueness give the same actual
endpoint, so its norm is at least one. If ℓm = 0, the endpoint is the identity and its norm
is one. The radius may be chosen δ −1 + 2 after fixing δ; all identities are fixed-parameter
instances. This proves Proposition 34.5.
    For zero covariance the drivers and covariance means vanish, and the claims reduce

its exact completed mean application. They provide no uniform control-radius budget,
stochastic moment upper bound or nonlinear product ratio by themselves.

   nonnegative mixed expansion and an independent convergent solution series. It cor-

   per-pairing majorant has exponent ρ > κ and cannot give the desired scale. This is a
