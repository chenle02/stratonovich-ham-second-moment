## Proposed conservation mechanism and main audit points

Free group conservation needs the exact full sine/cosine Fourier matrix,
with zero-frequency sine quotient and Bessel isometry. The pointwise
2x2 matrix has determinant1 and preserves the canonical skew form; an
integral argument on the real Fourier subspace, then E density, must
justify the operator identity. No finite determinant is a proof on E.

B(b) is bounded on E for smooth compact b and is omega-skew via real
multiplication symmetry. Conjugation by the free symplectic group makes
its interaction coefficient omega-skew as a bounded strong-state operator.
The ordinary source interaction evolution is statewise differentiable;
this is the planned way to handle arbitrary E inputs without assuming
unbounded generator differentiation or higher regularity of raw solutions.
These are future analytic proof obligations, not accepted SP statements.

For source plus completed mean convergence, the deterministic covariance
error is bounded by ||Phi|| times the actual L2 Gaussian operator error.
Uniform-in-rho deterministic driver norms follow likewise at FIXEDPhi.
Gaussian source tails plus deterministic shift and theta>1/2 provide the
same all-finite-moment domination for the Young source limit. This needs
an explicit proof, not a claim that shifted sources are centered Gaussian.
Source fields cY_rho+h_Phi,rho are generally NONCENTERED.

Raw preservation is stable under uniform operator-norm limits because the
bilinear form is bounded; use a subsequence at each fixed parameter tuple.
All time pairs may follow from uniform triangle convergence. No common
null set for all Phi,c,delta,R is required for the pointwise applications.
Norm>=1 follows without inverse; inverse must instead come from actual
Young/free evolution. This distinction matters in infinite dimensions.
