psi(t,x)=f(t)q(x), with f compactly supported in(0,1),q compactly supported
on R. The raw covariance profile is h(t,x)=k(t)(Gamma*q)(x), where
k(t)=integral_0^1 |t-s|^-a f(s)ds. Preserve the covariance amplitude and
cost v=E W(psi)^2; neither k nor its raw normalization is adjustable for free.
White space is an allowed first subcase, not the replacement objective.
For Riesz the spatial profile has a tail; compact spatial support cannot
be silently imported from the white subcase.

2. Extend its projection/dual functional boundedly to F, including both
   displacement and velocity and the nonzero projection of(1,0). An L2

4. Choose one sufficiently long fixed fast block, prove an invariant cone

   fractional block. Prove a nonzero terminal smooth averaged observable;

## Falsification before investment

Use a finite two-mode transfer test to refute the invalid implication
'large norm in each block implies growth of their product': consecutive
expanding directions may differ. Likewise a large phase norm can lie in
the kernel of the terminal displacement average. These elementary checks
are diagnostic targets, not counterexamples to the actual HAM.
A valid cone argument must prevent both failures with proved hypotheses
for the actual profiles, rather than assume the desired product rate.
