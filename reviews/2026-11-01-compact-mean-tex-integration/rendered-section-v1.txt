25         Established steps and open questions
1. The mixed expansion and the first obstruction. Section 3 proves the complete
   nonnegative mixed expansion and an independent convergent solution series. It cor-
   rects the time-uniformity issue in the source’s intermediate estimate. The common-
   per-pairing majorant has exponent ρ > κ and cannot give the desired scale. This is a
   restriction on that majorant, not on the model or all uses of the source’s Theorem 3.1.

2. Calibrations. Section 4 gives the finite white and Riesz coefficients. As α0 ↓ 0,
   the target exponent becomes (4 − α)/(3 − α), the static exponent. Proposition 2.4
   is unconditional; its numerical 2L∗ specialization requires the stated first-moment
   premise. Comparisons with the Skorohod model identify time exponents only.

3. The full upper scale. Section 5 gives explicit bounds when α ≤ 1. Section 13
   proves the full homogeneous-covariance upper scale by independent interval fields,
   local Gaussian-degree estimates and dyadic covariance domination. The latter proof
   does not assume an endpoint representation of the spatial covariance.

4. The sharp constant remains open. Goal 24.2 requires a lower bound beyond
   the unconditional Jensen comparison and a matching sharp upper constant. The
   new candidate in Section 14 supplies a static lower comparison for all admissible
   temporal exponents and a strict actual-mean gap only near the static endpoint; these
   proofs passed their own scoped review. A representation using two ordered chains
   is a possible research direction, not a proved result of these notes. The intended
   normalization of the source’s first-moment constants also remains to be clarified.
