135: \begin{hypothesis}[Homogeneous covariance]\label{H:homog}
136:   Let $d\in\{1,2,3\}$ and $0<\alpha_0<1$.
137:   The spatial covariance is a nonnegative, even, positive-definite
138:   tempered Radon measure $\Gamma$ on $\R^d$, with positive even
139:   tempered Radon spectral measure $\mu$ in the convention
140:   \[
141:     \Gamma=\int e^{i\xi\cdot x}\mu(\ud\xi)
142:     \quad\text{in the sense of tempered distributions}.
143:   \]
144:   The symbol $\gamma$ in~\eqref{E:cov} denotes this covariance as a
145:   function when one exists, and as a distribution otherwise.
146:   For every Borel set $A$ and $c>0$,
147:   $\Gamma(cA)=c^{d-\alpha}\Gamma(A)$, and
148:   \begin{equation}\label{E:threshold}
149:     \alpha_0+\alpha<2,
150:   \end{equation}
151:   with $0<\alpha<d$, or $\alpha=d=1$.
152:   In particular, the last case includes spatial white noise
153:   $\Gamma=\delta_0$.
154: \end{hypothesis}
