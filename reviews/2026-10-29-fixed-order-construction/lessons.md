# Durable lessons from the construction repair

- Prove existence separately from the quantitative asymptotic estimate. A
  source construction can be reconstructed at fixed covariance without
  importing its constants into the parameter-only asymptotic constant.
- Spectral Laplace transforms can be monotone while the physical heat
  covariances are not. Substitute common minimum widths only in the
  nonnegative transform, never pointwise in the spatial integrand.
- Homogeneity excludes positive horizon atoms but does not exclude
  coordinate-zero faces. Test those faces by a separate large Laplace
  parameter; keep the same-degree face-supported negative control.
- In Brownian occupation domination, fractional powers below k do not obey
  S^a <= S^k for small S. The safe bound is (1+S)^k. Record the actual
  two-horizon prefactor rather than relying on a printed normalization.
- Edition custody can be nonblocking: write the elementary finite-measure
  Laplace criterion directly instead of citing a theorem number from the
  wrong edition or waiting for ingestion.
- Consumer replacement must follow proof completion and covariance-class
  checking. A clean build, an old review, and a carrier receipt each cover
  different claims; none certifies the new mathematics.
