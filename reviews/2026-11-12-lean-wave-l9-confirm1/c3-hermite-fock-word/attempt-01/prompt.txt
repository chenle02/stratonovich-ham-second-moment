  (PASS: resolved and correct; PASS_WITH_NOTES: resolved, with a minor
  wording point reported as a minor finding; PARTIAL: only partly
  resolved; FAIL: not resolved or a new error; INCONCLUSIVE: not
  checkable). reason: evidence with file:line.
- "findings": fatal = misstatement a citing reader would be misled by;
  major = material imprecision; minor = wording. Each string starts with an
  ID (C1, C2, ...), names the item, quotes Lean and TeX with file:line,
  and gives the exact fix.
- "required_repairs": only changes that must be made before these rows can
  be cited as correct.
- "source_coverage": every file read, exact line ranges, "complete" or
  "partial".
- "limitations": anything you could not check.
- "verdict": PASS or PASS_WITH_NOTES only if fatal, major and
  required_repairs are empty and every check is PASS or PASS_WITH_NOTES;
  FAIL if any fatal or major finding or required repair; INCONCLUSIVE if
  the reading requirement could not be met.
- "confidence": high only if requirement A is complete.
