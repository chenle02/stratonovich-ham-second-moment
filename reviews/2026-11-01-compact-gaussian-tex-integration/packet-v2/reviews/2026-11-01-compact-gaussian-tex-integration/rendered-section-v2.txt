       Sobolev compactness
We prove the finite Gaussian and compact-cutoff estimates needed to construct the
wave driver. All traces and Schatten norms in this section are unnormalized. The spatial
states remain on Rd , d ∈ {1, 2, 3}. Here p is an auxiliary Schatten and Gaussian-moment
index. The auxiliary periodic space below is used only to factor a compactly supported
multiplication operator. No infinite Gaussian series or time path is constructed in this
section.
