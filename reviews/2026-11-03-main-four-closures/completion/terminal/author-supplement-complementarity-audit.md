## Main finding

The replacement is **not mathematically equivalent as a collection of supplemental worked derivations**. Several removed files substantially overlap the new driver/application closure, but some contain complementary calculations that the new closure expressly consumes as local inputs or abbreviates. The clearest example is old `driver-variance-interval.txt:49–150`: it derives the actual two-entry Fourier kernel, covariance normalization, Hilbert-valued Bessel factor4, and the reflected second variance. New `driver-path-source-full-closure.txt:13–31` calls073/074 proved typed wave inputs and explicitly says it asserts no new Fourier-row theorem. New Cameron/tube §8 also consumes those variances. Neither restates the old calculation at that depth.

This is a supplement-depth loss, not an omitted own-source proof: those exact source proofs are still in both carriers and remain assigned. Conversely the new four add substantial independent-upper, path/source, Cameron/tube and positive-matching work; their mathematical value is not reduced by this inventory finding. Titles, byte counts, and the phrase “overlapping” cannot establish equivalence in either direction.
