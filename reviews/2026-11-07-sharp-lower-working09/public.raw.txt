## 0.1 Singular invariant-direction covariance

Throughout the admissible singular class

\[

# PROOF112 — `P:compact-ground-family`

### Imported Teschl facts, actually located in the given pages

- **Theorem 10.2, p. 258 (`F153`).** For \(d\le3\), real \(V\in L^\infty_\infty(\R^d)+L^2(\R^d)\) is relatively compact with respect to \(-\Delta\); \(H=H_0+V\) is self-adjoint on \(H^2\), bounded below, and \(\sigma_{\mathrm{ess}}(H)=[0,\infty)\); \(C_c^\infty\) is a core. Here each \(V_\sigma\in C_c^\infty\subset L^2\), so this applies directly and supplies the essential threshold.
- **Theorem 10.13, p. 273 (`F154–F155`).** If the bottom of the spectrum of \(H\) is an eigenvalue, it is simple with a strictly positive ground state.
- **Theorem 4.12 and Corollary 4.13, pp. 140–141 (`F147–F149`).** Max–min/min–max levels \(E_j(H)\) are computed below the essential spectrum, and \(A\ge B\) in PSD order implies \(E_j(A)\ge E_j(B)\); the page explicitly allows the quadratic-form domain in the definition of \(U(\psi_1,\ldots,\psi_{n-1})\).
- **Theorem 2.14, p. 81 (`F139`).** A closed semi-bounded form is the form of a unique self-adjoint operator; this justifies the \(H^1\) form-domain identification.

### Level bound, simplicity, and positivity

For the shift \(c=1+M\), \(A_\sigma=H_\sigma+c\). Since \(V_\sigma\le M\),

\[
0\le M-V_\sigma\le M,
\qquad
B=1-\Delta,\qquad B\le A_\sigma\le (1+M)B.
\]

The common form domain is \(H^1\), because on \(H^2\):

\[
\|A_\sigma^{1/2}u\|_2^2
=q_\sigma(u)+c\|u\|_2^2
=\|u\|_{H^1}^2-\int V_\sigma|u|^2+M\|u\|_2^2,
\]

whence \(q_\sigma\) is form-bounded below by \(-M\|\cdot\|_2^2\) and the completed form domain is \(H^1\). The trial (E:compact-common-trial) gives \(E_1(\sigma)\le-\varepsilon<0\), and the \(L^\infty\) bound of \(V_\sigma\) gives \(E_1(\sigma)\ge-M\). Hence

\[
-M\le E_1(\sigma)\le-\varepsilon<0=\inf\sigma_{\mathrm{ess}}(H_\sigma).
\]

Therefore \(E_1(\sigma)\) is an isolated eigenvalue of finite multiplicity, and Theorem 10.13 makes it **simple** with a real strictly positive normalized ground state \(p_\sigma\).

### Level continuity and gap

For unit \(u\), \(|q_\sigma(u)-q_\tau(u)|\le\|V_\sigma-V_\tau\|_\infty\int|u|^2\), and the max–min formula

\[
E_j=\sup_{\psi_1,\ldots,\psi_{j-1}}
\inf_{u\in U(\psi_1,\ldots,\psi_{j-1}),\|u\|_2=1}q(u)
\]

makes each \(E_j\) a \(1\)-Lipschitz function in \(\|\cdot\|_\infty\). With \(E_2(\sigma)\) set to zero if there is no second negative level,

\[
|E_j(\sigma)-E_j(\tau)|\le\|V_\sigma-V_\tau\|_\infty,\qquad j=1,2.
\]

Simplicity and isolation give \(E_2-E_1>0\) pointwise; continuity on the compact interval \(I\) gives \(\Delta_*:=\min_I(E_2-E_1)>0\). With \(\lambda=\sqrt{-E_1}\), \(\mu=\sqrt{-E_2}\),

\[
\lambda_\sigma-\mu_\sigma
=\frac{E_2-E_1}{\lambda+\mu}
\ge\frac{\Delta_*}{2\sqrt M}=:d_0>0,
\]

never dividing by \(\mu_\sigma\) (which may be zero).

### Ground-state continuity in \(L^2\) and \(H^2\)

Decompose \(p_\tau=a p_\sigma+r\), \(r\perp p_\sigma\), \(a>0\), \(a^2+\|r\|_2^2=1\). Set \(d_V=\|V_\sigma-V_\tau\|_\infty\). Then

\[
q_\sigma(p_\tau)\le q_\tau(p_\tau)+d_V
=E_1(\tau)+d_V\le E_1(\sigma)+2d_V,
\]

and the complementary max–min bound gives

\[
q_\sigma(p_\tau)=a^2E_1(\sigma)+q_\sigma(r)
\ge a^2E_1(\sigma)+E_2(\sigma)\|r\|_2^2
=E_1(\sigma)+(E_2-E_1)\|r\|_2^2.
\]

Hence \(\|r\|_2^2\le2d_V/\Delta_*\), and

\[
\|p_\tau-p_\sigma\|_2^2=2(1-a)\le2(1-a^2)=2\|r\|_2^2
\le\frac{4d_V}{\Delta_*}.
\]

Finally the eigen-equation

\[
(1-\Delta)p_\sigma=(1+V_\sigma-\lambda_\sigma^2)p_\sigma
\]

has a right side continuous in \(L^2\), and \((1-\Delta)^{-1}\) is an isometry onto the Bessel \(H^2\), giving continuity and boundedness of \(p_\sigma\) in \(H^2\).

# PROOF131 — `L:variational-control-dilation`
