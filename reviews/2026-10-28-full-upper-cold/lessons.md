# Lessons from the first complete cold read

Scoped correctness and whole-paper readability are different gates. A
skeptic can reconstruct an argument at HIGH while a fresh referee cannot
follow its compressed presentation. Honest confusion at a load-bearing
step is substantive; cite neither the old verdict nor the absence of a
counterexample as a reason to clear it. Expand, review the changed source,
and obtain a fresh cold read.

A global source caveat does not condition an unqualified local theorem.
Keep unconditional Jensen/Cauchy--Schwarz deductions separate from their
numerical L_star consequence. State the precise first-moment premise at
every numerical consumer while retaining the original sharp research goal.

Verify actual delivery at the provider boundary. The exported initial
message contained every manuscript proof and the bibliography. This is
stronger than a manifest or a model claiming it read the paper. Source
availability is weaker than consultation, and partial consultation is weaker
than a complete source audit. Preserve exact native ranges and limitations.

An isolated git root plus explicit --dir and matching cwd fixed the earlier
tool re-anchoring failure. Test a harmless packet-local read before the real
dispatch. Existing runtime/account configuration stayed unchanged. A larger
launch-local output cap avoided the earlier no-report length failure; the
new attempt ended with a real free-form report and final stop in400.983s.

Do not blindly adopt a referee's suggested replacement citation. The Chen2023
import is the finite variation in the closing sentence of Theorem1.1 for
its(1.5), which directly yields the normalized quartic bound. Its homogeneous
Corollary1.2 is not needed. Improve the actual pinpoint and preserve the
valid mathematical input rather than changing the theorem being invoked.
