## Noncritical findings

1. **PROOF113 dual-norm wording.** The dual of `‖A_σ^{1/4}u‖` is stated at `z ∈ L²`, but `‖A_σ^{-1/4}z‖` is only finite on the completing class `D(A_σ^{-1/4}) = H^{-1/2}`. The argument is correct after completion (Cauchy–Schwarz gives the upper bound; `u` a scalar multiple of `A_σ^{-1/2}z` attains the norm), but the printed sentence is imprecise.

2. **PROOF114 graph-level bookkeeping is compressed.** The claim that the spectral matrix gives continuous `H²×H¹` solutions is correct but the key bound `√(h+c)·S_t(h)` bounded uniformly in `h ≥ −M` (equivalently `A_σ^{1/2}S_t(H_σ) ∈ B(L²)` with bound `C(1+t)`) is not written out.

3. **OCRt-only source pinpoints.** Teschl pp. 140–141 (max–min), p. 258 (Thm 10.2), p. 273 (Thm 10.13) and Friz–Hairer p. 62 ((4.3)) were checked against the embedded text carriers only. No rendered page images are in this snapshot, so typeset-level pinpoints remain uncertified. This is an evidence limitation, not a mathematical defect.

4. **PROOF117/118 rely on the upstream raw covariance completion.** The identities `E[W(Ψ)W(F₀)] = ∫F₀(s,x)h_s(x)dx ds` and `v_Ψ = E Z² = ∫₀¹∫Ψ(σ,x)h_σ(x)dx dσ` are imported from the covariance/raw-noise/source machinery (Hypothesis H:homog together with the temporal completion `L:masked-time-completion` and the spatial/spectral completion `P:masked-noise-completion`), not re-proven inside the compact-mean module. PROOF117's own contribution — reducing `E[W(Ψ)W(F₀)]` to the double integral of `|σ−u|^{-a}` against the spatial Γ-pairing via the raw covariance identity and absolute Fubini — is correct under `a = α₀ < 1` and one compact spatial support, and the temporal factor `2/((1−a)(2−a))` is exact. But the antecedent fact that `|·|^{-a}` is positive semidefinite (so the completion exists) and that the pairing holds for non-separable smooth compact `Ψ` is an upstream given. This is a genuine dependency, correctly identified by scope ("covariance/raw-noise supply the completion"); it is not an internal defect, but it is recorded as not internally re-proven in this audit.

5. **Downstream consumers out of scope.** The raw-block lower bound (`raw-block-consumer.tex`), the unmasked variational rate and score supremum (`variational-consumer.tex`), and the canonical scaling (`scaling-context.tex`) are actual downstream context but are not certified by this unit. ## Checked derivations

**PROOF112 (P:compact-ground-family).** (i) Form identity: on `H²`, `‖A_σ^{1/2}u‖₂² = q_σ(u)+c‖u‖₂²` with `c=1+M`; integration by parts plus `0 ≤ V_σ ≤ M` gives `q_B ≤ q_{A_σ} ≤ c q_B`, hence `‖u‖_{H¹}² ≤ ‖A^{1/2}u‖² ≤ c‖u‖_{H¹}²`. Spectral cutoffs `1_{[1,n]}(A_σ)` show `H²` is a core for the half-power norm; Fourier cutoffs show density in `H¹`; since both norms dominate `L²`, the two completions coincide with `H¹`. (ii) `−M ≤ E₁ ≤ −ε`: lower bound via `q_σ ≥ ‖∇u‖² − M‖u‖² ≥ −M`; upper via the common trial. `E₁ < 0 = σ_ess` makes it an isolated eigenvalue of finite multiplicity *before* Thm 10.13 is invoked. (iii) `|E_j(σ)−E_j(τ)| ≤ ‖V_σ−V_τ‖_∞` by the sup-inf max–min formula on one common form domain; the bound `|q_σ(u)−q_τ(u)| ≤ ‖V_σ−V_τ‖_∞` for unit `u` transfers through sup and inf; the threshold case `E₂ = 0` remains in the same formula, so the Lipschitz bound passes through the threshold. (iv) `Δ_* = min_I(E₂−E₁) > 0`, and `λ−μ = (E₂−E₁)/(λ+μ) ≥ Δ_*/(2√M)` since `λ+μ ≤ 2√M`; no division by `μ`. (v) Ground continuity: decompose `p_τ = a p_σ + r`, `r ⊥ p_σ`, `0<a≤1` (positivity of both states, Cauchy–Schwarz); `q_σ(p_τ) ≤ E₁(σ)+2d_V` and `q_σ(p_τ) ≥ E₁ + Δ_*‖r‖²` (the cross form-term `q_σ(p_σ,r)` vanishes because `p_σ` is an eigenfunction); hence `‖r‖² ≤ 2d_V/Δ_*`. Then `‖p_τ−p_σ‖² = (1−a)²+‖r‖² = 2(1−a)` (using `‖r‖²=1−a²`), and `2(1−a) ≤ 2(1−a²) = 2‖r‖² ≤ 4d_V/Δ_*`. All exact. (vi) `(1−Δ)p_σ = (1−λ_σ²+V_σ)p_σ` (from `−Δp = (V_σ−λ²)p`); the right side is `L²`-continuous with bound `1+2M`; `(1−Δ)^{-1}` bounded `L²→H²` gives the asserted `H²` continuity and bound. **Status: PASS.**
