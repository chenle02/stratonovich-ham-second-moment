# Lessons retained during the interval-field attempt

The full argument passed all11 independent checks/HIGH, with full source
delivery and no required repairs; see adjudication-v1.md. This is scoped
correctness evidence, not formal verification, novelty or circulation clearance.
The observations below distinguish actual diagnostics, accepted prerequisites
and still-proposed analytic extensions.

1. Retain the ordinary Stratonovich coefficient. Gaussian Hermite truncation
   is a computational device, not Wick normal ordering. Vacuum words require
   both creation and annihilation. The static prerequisite proves this;
   local cutoffs for multiple interval colors passed their new application check.
2. Do not spend a fine-interval loss twice without checking its summability.
   The executed first-order inventory example has a bounded refined sum and
   a divergent sum with uniformly squared metric losses. The candidate
   separates a two-appearance mean on one phase copy from a one-appearance
   cross field on each copy. That distinction, rather than an independence
   assertion about time pieces, is the reviewed repair.
3. Time ordering and counting must be proved together. The executed
   noncommuting disjoint/nested/overlapping two-color examples agree with
   the time-sorted cube coefficient and retain all nine degree splits.
   They do not prove the arbitrary-color insertion estimate or inventory bound.
4. Arbitrary-phase coefficients have no automatic positivity. Some of those
   nine finite energy splits are negative. Gaussian parity deletes odd total
   color degree; it does not make the remaining coefficients positive.
   The candidate uses absolute bounds there and reserves covariance
   domination for the positive constant-data wave pairing integrands.
5. Compare at fixed order before taking noise limits. The current author
   source supplies a fixed-order L2 limit and an independently convergent
   original series. A uniform interval-model moment estimate does not itself
   prove full mollified-solution convergence. The proposed transfer keeps
   these obligations separate and supplies the actual source pages.
6. A correct static exponent is not a new theorem by itself. Static
   exponents are already known. Distinguish arbitrary-phase operator control,
   the new colored-time application, sharp constants, correctness review,
   formal verification and novelty. None substitutes for another.
7. Runtime identity belongs to the review evidence. A new default CLI can
   invalidate image-ledger support. Account wrappers may ignore an explicitly
   named binary; here the preflight refused that mismatch before dispatch.
   A launch-local PATH selected the already installed supported runtime and
   doctor verified the effective executable/account. No persistent setup
   change or review-tool modification was needed.
