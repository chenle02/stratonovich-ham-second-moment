# Durable lessons

- Covariance Loewner order does not imply positivity of an ordinary wave
  mean's self-pair functional. Test the actual projected coefficient, not
  only PSD of the residual or a generic Gaussian density identity.
- Normalize an actual noise-Hilbert projection. A chosen positive profile
  without its Gaussian covariance/variance normalization is not a projection.
- Lift a coefficient counterexample to a full-mean claim only with a uniform
  small-coupling remainder. Here the parent's positive mixed series supplies
  it, and the finite-coordinate density transfers it to the modified law.
- Read the source's chosen construction. The popular time-convolution example
  in its introduction is not its actual independent-Laplace-slice cutoff.
  Cross-covariance with the fixed full coordinate has a single spatial heat
  width, while the field's own covariance has twice that width.
- Keep the coordinate fixed across regularizations. Width-dependent coordinate
  normalization would change the tilted law and invalidate the limiting map.
- Adjudicate incidental review assertions as well as listed findings. A HIGH
  review is not authority to adopt its incorrect blanket pointwise claim.
- Reuse exact kernel deductions for arithmetic, while reviewing the Gaussian,
  integral and source application separately. Neither review claims execution.
- A small-coupling residual-discard failure is not an all-tilts or large-time
  obstruction. The missing actual-model lower rate still needs a new bridge.
