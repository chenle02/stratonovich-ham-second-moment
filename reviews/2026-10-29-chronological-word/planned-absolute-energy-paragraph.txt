Proposed replacement after the entire-majorant display:

The majorant converges uniformly on every compact amplitude polydisc,
so it defines an entire vector function. Coefficientwise absolute
convergence can be seen directly. Fix $z_*>0$ and put
\[
  K_{2z_*}=\sup_{\max_i|z_i|\le2z_*}
  \|\mathcal U(T;\boldsymbol z)v\|_{\mathcal H_{1/T}}<\infty.
\]
This follows by taking the temporary phase parameter $\lambda=1/T$
in the majorant. Normalized vector Cauchy integration on that larger
polydisc gives
\[
  \|U_{\boldsymbol r}v\|_{\mathcal H_{1/T}}
  \le K_{2z_*}(2z_*)^{-|\boldsymbol r|},\qquad
  \sum_{\boldsymbol r}z_*^{|\boldsymbol r|}
  \|U_{\boldsymbol r}v\|_{\mathcal H_{1/T}}
  \le2^{|\mathcal F|}K_{2z_*}.
\]
The sum is the product of $|\mathcal F|$ geometric series. Consequently,
Cauchy--Schwarz bounds the absolute energy double series by
$4^{|\mathcal F|}K_{2z_*}^2$. This finite factor is used only to justify
the coefficient identities at a fixed family and positive smoothing;
it does not enter the uniform coefficient estimate below.
