# Calibration and falsification evidence

cone-controls-v1.json was generated by check-cone-v1.py using mpmath.
It is evidence about geometry, observable and scaling choices, not a
certified numerical enclosure, stochastic estimate or analytical proof.

Nine direct radial H1 scaling rows cover d1/2/3 and R1/2/5. Four actual d1
free-wave d'Alembert cases at horizons4/8/16/32 give average squared value1
with data radius L+2, versus0 with radius1. A normalized smooth potential
pulse outside fixed mask radius1 but inside the horizon6 cone contributes
first-Picard .5 in the full equation and0 in the masked equation. Three
heat/pointmass rows show that physical masking and heat smoothing do not
commute. The pointmass here is an auxiliary deterministic distribution,
not an actual Gaussian source sample. Three stationary harmonic Gaussian
toy rows distinguish expected average of squares1 from expected square
of average sinc(k)^2. This harmonic covariance is NOT the original
homogeneous class. Twelve delta-log polynomial-data rows are diagnostic;
their limit still requires the explicit analytic argument.

These controls reject fixed-data/fixed-mask substitution, smoothing a
masked noise, and average-versus-square confusion. They do NOT establish
raw moment equality, cone uniqueness for rough noise, uniform operator
bounds, a sharp constant, or source normalization.
