# Durable lessons from the lower-comparison wave

- Compare raw time powers on the SAME positive spatial pairing measure.
  Exponent-dependent Laplace cutoffs do not interpolate by the same power law.
- Define the static time exponent separately. It is covariance1,not Gamma_E(0).
- A temporal covariance minorant T^-a is a VARIANCE strength;the noise amplitude
  is T^(-a/2). Keeping this distinction gives the correct asymptotic clock.
- The static endpoint norm series can be constructed from a fixed positive
  fractional-time upper bound via finite positive Wick comparison and geometric
  tails. This avoids borrowing an unrelated source's time-uniform estimate.
- Positive entire-series growth controls a coefficient LIMSUP type. Do not
  infer an exact coefficient limit or exclude zero/oscillating subsequences.
- A strict Eu-to-Eu2rate gap does not automatically prove a theorem for E|u|
  or full moment intermittency. Name the observable and near-static window.
- An independent review may add an optional numerical aside from a disputed
  corollary. Retain the report but do not adopt that aside as a proof input.
- Treat novelty and validity separately. A source corollary can be useful for
  the goal while remaining unsuitable as an independently claimed new theorem.
- A case-sensitive contract filename must match the supplied workspace exactly.
  Fix the name before dispatch,preserve the refusal,and do not waive it.
