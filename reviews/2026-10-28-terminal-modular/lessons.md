Durable lessons from the two whole-paper cold reads

- State the ordinary two-chain terminal integral before any reversal or
  comparison. Label both time domains and endpoints, include empty chains,
  and derive each Wick coefficient. Gaussian degree includes annihilation
  and self-pairings; it is not homogeneous Wick-chaos degree.
- Give a fixed-degree-split estimate its own lemma before an inventory sum.
  Expose the number of used copies in each metric factor. A reader should
  be able to check the bound without reconstructing it from a table.
- If positive mixed moments and uniform scaled finite-model bounds are
  already available, use a larger amplitude to dominate each term and its
  tail. This can prove full-series convergence from a fixed-order import
  without invoking a stronger external series theorem. Keep the larger
  amplitude for tails and the original amplitude for the final constant.
- Scoped correctness acceptance does not resolve a whole-paper reader's
  substantive assembly concerns. Substantial added lemmas require fresh
  review, even when the theorem and earlier operator estimates are unchanged.
- Providing source files does not establish that a reviewer consulted them.
  Record actual source coverage, including zero tool calls, separately from
  the reviewer's mathematical concerns and from native byte delivery.

These are project-local workflow lessons. They do not certify the current
candidate mathematics or authorize changes to shared skills or repositories.

Third cold-read lesson: giving a compressed argument its own lemma does not
make its count checkable. When the claim depends on charging each operator
norm change once, write the chronological partition, intermediate spaces,
identity maps, normalized coefficient integrals and a concrete constant.
Finite endpoint-budget checks can falsify a proposed charging rule;they do
not replace the word-norm proof. A new HIGH scoped report cannot erase a
fresh reader's unresolved inability to follow that proof.

attempt. Preserve the existing report and use a new source-bound round for
