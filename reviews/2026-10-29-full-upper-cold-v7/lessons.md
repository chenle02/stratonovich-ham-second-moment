# Durable lessons

- A cold reader's unease about a long proof is not a verified failed inequality.
  Preserve the concern and exact existing independent checks;do not run an
  unchanged review until a preferred verdict appears.
- Separate mathematical scope from journal genre. A full-scope upper theorem
  can have a compact proof map while historical/conditional routes remain in
  research notes. Removing an original goal to improve a report is not repair.
- Check exact byte provenance before claiming a critical proof is unchanged.
  Three critical proofs match actual primary bytes;the interval proposition
  has an already recorded singleton explanation at final adoption. State both.
- Authenticate published numbers from the published PDF. The local source TeX
  has the mathematical statement but cannot certify publisher page/equation
  numbers. Both citations were already in the corpus;no duplicate intake needed.
- Actual source tools govern coverage. One invalid regex was repaired;it is
  not evidence of an inaccessible paper. A report opening that implies all
  source versions were consulted does not establish an unobserved v1reading.
- Whole-page wiki summaries can retain a superseded exact-limit sentence even
  after the theorem paragraph is corrected. Scan all comparison prose.
- Gaussian-tilt shortcuts must retain the effect they need. Discarding the
  residual Gaussian mean can lose the leading factor;this restricted scalar
  observation must not be inflated into a no-go for all Cameron--Martin routes.
