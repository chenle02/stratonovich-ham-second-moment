# Durable lessons from the sixth cold cycle

- A current-state header must be refreshed,not merely followed by thousands
  of appended log lines. Progress.md's old second-cold snapshot was stale;
  its header now reflects actual source-bound states and remaining work.
- Audit the exact proof of a disputed imported construction. A dependency
  graph is not the same as checking every intermediate formula. Separate
  repairable proof-line errors from a false theorem.
- Physical heat covariances need not be pointwise monotone. Positive spectral
  Laplace transforms can be monotone even when their densities are not.
- Homogeneity excludes fixed positive-level horizon atoms by scaled disjoint
  copies,but does NOT by itself exclude coordinate-zero faces. Check those
  separately by sending the corresponding Laplace parameter to infinity.
- Preserve the original class and object explicitly in the formal hypothesis
  and theorem. State the already-prescribed canonical expansion;do not invent
  uniqueness or choose a different easier target.
- A filename/canonical-host presence count does not authenticate an edition.
  The kallenberg-02PDF is actually Third Edition on BOTH hosts. Preserve bytes,
  queue correct-edition custody,and never transplant an old theorem number.
- Falsification scripts and algebra controls are diagnostics,not independent
  review or proofs of infinite-dimensional convergence.
