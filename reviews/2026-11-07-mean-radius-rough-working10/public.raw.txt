- \(K\) on \(L^2(I)\), kernel \(|s-t|^{-a}\), is shown **compact, self-adjoint, strictly positive, injective** by truncated-kernel Hilbert–Schmidt approximation plus Schur control of the remainder:

  \]
- Strict positivity uses the Gamma representation of \(|v|^{-a}\) and the strict quadratic form of \(e^{-\lambda|v|}\).
- Non-trace-classity is shown directly: \(L^2\)-orthonormal cell indicators have summed quadratic forms
  \[
  2N^a/((1-a)(2-a))\to\infty.
  \]
  So \(K^{1/2}\iota:H_t\to L^2\) is asserted and checked **not** Hilbert–Schmidt.
- The dimension-free transfer to \(L^r\), \(2<r<2/a\), is done through the convolution/Hölder estimates, then covariance Cauchy–Schwarz and \(L^r\) duality:
  \[
  \|Kf\|_{L^r}\le C_{a,r}\|f\|_{H_t}.
  \]
  This is legitimate: the positive-kernel HLS/Young route gives
  \[
  \|g\|_{H_t}^2\le\|K_a\|_{r/2}\|g\|_{r'}^2,

  \]
  and `F016` explicitly verifies the required Young inequality with the split-monomial Hölder proof.
- `F016` `P:cameron-full-mean` then proves **full fixed-\(R\) compactness** of

  T\Phi(s)=T_{\mathrm{sp}}(\mathcal U\Phi(s))
