# Durable lessons from the mild-construction repair

- A source dependency map must reach the actual consumer. Fixed-order
  convergence being independent of a faulty uniform estimate does not by
  itself justify a mild-equation identification that uses that estimate.
- Keep the corrected exponential factor through arbitrary vertexwise widths.
  The bound needed for the onefold integral is a mixed-width bound,not just
  a common-width solution moment.
- The external Gaussian factor is coupled to the inner polynomial. Its
  multiplication costs the occupied degree;the vacuum bound fails already
  for F=g and externalg,with squared norm3instead of1.
- Finite Gaussian degree is closed under the fixed-order L2 limit. This and
  creation/annihilation bounds can justify products without importing an
  unrelated model's hypercontractive moment theorem.
- A fixed-external-width variance may diverge when that width is removed.
  Use it to define the product only;get uniform outer-limit tails from the
  separate mixed-vertex estimate.
- Joint stationarity turns fixed-time L2 approximation error into a spatially
  uniform error. This supplies continuous representatives for Green-measure
  integration in dimension3;keep the Green/noise limits in their actual order.
- Carrier preparation and scientific acceptance have separate gates. A
  format-invalid calibration can retain objective complete native byte
  evidence;it is still invalid/unaccepted and never a science vote. A new
  scientific run must satisfy every normal report/coverage/HIGH requirement.

These are project-local lessons,not a global skill change or proof acceptance.
No helpful new reference absent from the six-repository corpus was needed.
