   nonnegative mixed expansion and an independent convergent solution series. It cor-
   rects the time-uniformity issue in the source’s intermediate estimate. The common-
   per-pairing majorant has exponent ρ > κ and cannot give the desired scale. This is a
   restriction on that majorant, not on the model or all uses of the source’s Theorem 3.1.
