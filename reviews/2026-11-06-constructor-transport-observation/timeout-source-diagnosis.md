Thus this is a deadline to the HTTPresponse/fetch stage (normally headers), NOT a600000ms cap on the whole subsequently consumed streamed answer.

Undici7.27.1 defaults both headersTimeout and bodyTimeout to3e5 (300000ms). Its H1parser installs the header timer before response headers, switches to bodyTimeout on final headers, and refreshes the body timer on data. The supported Kimi knob sets ONLY headersTimeout: it neither raises the separate SDK600000ms fetch/header timer nor the body-idle300000ms timeout. Backend/proxy/service deadlines are also outside this control and were not probed.

Consequently1200000 is a VALID env value but DOES NOT establish a20-minute effective header/whole-request allowance here. A future headers setting can remove the earlier5-minute Undiciheader limit while the unchanged SDK still bounds header waiting at about10minutes. A streaming body that has started is instead subject to body-idle/transport behavior; the SDKfetch timer has already been cleared. This distinction is proved by the actual setTimeout/clearTimeout source, not documentation wording alone.

Both SDKs classify a fetch error or its cause matching timeout into APIConnectionTimeoutError; Kimi maps that to its APITimeoutError. The generic message has lost the lower-level cause, so the present evidence does NOT prove that every timeout was specifically an Undiciheader timeout rather than upstream/proxy behavior.
