\begin{hypothesis}[Homogeneity]\label{H:homog}
  $\gamma(cx)=c^{-\alpha}\gamma(x)$ for $c>0$, and
  \begin{equation}\label{E:threshold}
    \alpha_0+\alpha<2,
  \end{equation}
  with $0<\alpha<d$, or $\alpha=d=1$ (spatial white noise).
\end{hypothesis}


The standard three-dimensional wave measure is sigma_a/(4pi a),where sigma_a is ordinary surface-area measure on the sphere of radius a;hat G_a(xi)=sin(a|xi|)/|xi|,with the continuous value a at0. The new proof states its full covariance/spectral/Gaussian conventions explicitly. Fourier transform is unnormalized int exp(-i xi.x)f(x)dx.
