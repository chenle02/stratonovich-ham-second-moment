# Durable proof-writing lessons from the fourth cold read

- Keep the original all-parameter form estimate until each use is justified.
  Optimizing for the imaginary part can lose the spectral-shift real-part
  bound. Display both parameter choices.
- Prove exact cutoff preservation prefix by prefix. Count ordinary field
  insertions in each colour; do not replace them by final chaos degrees.
  Lowering operators and their self-contractions must survive.
- An input can depend on earlier colours while being in a fresh colour's
  vacuum. Use tensor first-chaos orthogonality,not informal conditioning.
- A cutoff identity for one finite coefficient is not an identity of full
  propagators at fixed cutoff.
- Expand a whole increasing product before length substitution. Bounding its
  inverse-length factor separately reverses the useful inequality.
- Distinguish (4.4),a mixed limit,from(5.4),a uniform estimate. Imported source
  theorem scope and its dependency audit are different from re-proving it.
- Adjudicate factual reviewer misattributions too. Do not add a nonexistent
  citation dependency to agree with a report.

These are project-local lessons,not mathematical acceptance or global skill
changes. No new references absent from the six-repository corpus were found.
