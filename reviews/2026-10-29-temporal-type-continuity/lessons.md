# Durable lessons

- Interpolate ALL chain splits on one disjoint-union positive measure.
  One mean orientation and two p2empty orientations are different counts.
- Positive coefficient type yields stronger rate log-convexity than merely
  applying Holder to the whole function. Keep the factorial power fixed.
- A coefficient LIMSUP premise does not supply a root Tendsto or eventual
  lower envelope. Existing stronger Lean theorems are partial reuse only.
- Distinguish upper growth-type continuity from existence of the time limit.
  The lower type is squeezed only at the static endpoint,with iterated limits.
- A complete abstract counterexample can stop a scalar shortcut without
  refuting the SPDE. Scope the obstruction to the hypotheses it actually tests.
- Coordinate existing Lean packages before creating another library. Exact
  types reveal useful component fits and prevent false full-claim reuse.
- Verify full source pins and hashes;historical receipts and evidence-successor
  commits are separate. API equality does not constitute a fresh gate replay.
- Incomplete registry coverage,including missing checkouts or omitted hits,
  cannot establish absence. Do not clone or register a duplicate from a zero hit.
