# Durable lessons from the energy-fidelity and literature checkpoint

## Existing rules retrieved too late

The initial active-phase recon used project/raw-text searches before the
actual synthesized wiki CLI. The existing wiki-first-recon-then-enrich rule
already covered this. This is an application failure,not a new workflow
discovery;no duplicate canonical skill was created. Project AGENTS now puts
CLI search/cite plus reading the returned pages directly beside literature
work. The completed queries and useful/irrelevant hits are preserved in
covariance-recon/wiki-cli-recon.json and wiki-recon.md. The Chen2023 wiki
page identified the exact quartic-bound lead;the pinned primary source,not
the generic concept page,supplied the Fourier convention and theorem scope.

## Queue provenance is a state machine

Le explicitly authorized helpful missing-reference retention. Four published-
DOI tickets were enqueued after DOI/key inventories and separate Papis lookup.
A request is queued,a claimed record is being processed,and completed gold
ingestion needs the exact ID/DOI receipt plus per-repository gates/convergence.
The first paper has that receipt in reference-state.json;the other three did
not at that snapshot. Raw arXiv strings are not sufficient repo-queue check
identities. Use idempotent UIDs;do not re-enqueue merely because the local
Dropbox checkout has not yet advanced to the canonical wiki commit.

## Scope restrictions travel with references

Read the exact synthesized paper page and then the primary theorem before
importing a result. Integer-dimensional star-body representation results do
not automatically apply to arbitrary homogeneous covariance measures with
a real exponent in dimension three. A quadratic-confinement probability-
measure minimizer does not identify the Sobolev-gradient functional used
here. Those restrictions are in the queued requests,not only this chat.

earlier failed tool call cannot gate a later independent call. At source
2718647,the guard verified all nineteen committed packet members and the
post-hook manuscript/PDF hashes before dispatch. That is byte provenance,

the required inverse row. A nonsymmetric tilt refused it;the correction is
recorded before freeze. Keep nonorthogonal/nonsymmetric examples in future

This is a finite diagnostic lesson,not a proof of the analytic theorem.
