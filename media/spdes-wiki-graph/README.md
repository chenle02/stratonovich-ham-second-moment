# SPDEs-wiki graph

`spdes-wiki-graph.png` shows the global graph view of the SPDEs-wiki, the
knowledge base on stochastic partial differential equations that the language
models write and maintain. Each dot is one of its more than 4,000 pages; the
image shows no page titles. It is shown in the preface, next to the paragraph that pictures
the research environment as a car.

It is the same image as in episode 10 of the video series, copied unchanged.
The source commit, capture details, licence and SHA-256 hash are in
`PROVENANCE.json`.

`spdes-wiki-local-book.png` shows the wiki around this book: the 28 pages that
link to the book's project page or that it links to, with the links among them.
It is drawn from the wiki's link index (not a screenshot, since the graph view
cannot label its dots), in a print layout with labels at 7.6 pt at the text
width. Some pages are linked only for a side remark. It was shown in the preface
after the paragraph on looking references up instead of recalling them, until
the graph of the stochastic-wave-equation slice took its place. It is
the print version, on a white background, of a figure made for the video series;
four long titles are shortened, as listed in `PROVENANCE.json`.

`wiki-slice-wave-equation.pdf` shows the wiki around the stochastic wave equation: 268
pages on three rings around this book, with the 2,230 links among them, every
page labelled. It is shown in the preface on a page of its own, where it took the
place of `spdes-wiki-local-book.png`; that earlier close-up is kept here but is
no longer shown in the book. At the printed width the labels are about 2 pt high
and are read by zooming the PDF. Details are in `PROVENANCE.json`.
