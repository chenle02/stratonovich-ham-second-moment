\subsection{Exact first-moment asymptotics}
\label{S:future-first-moment}

For a nonzero spatial covariance and a fixed positive temporal exponent
$\alpha_0$, can we determine the exact large-time growth of the signed mean
$\E u(t,x)$? This question is distinct
from determining the growth of $\E|u(t,x)|$. The current author version of
Xia Chen's paper gives two-sided bounds for the signed mean and predicts
that the upper bound is sharp;
see~\cite{chen:author2026:time-dependency}*{Theorem~1.2,
(1.10), the discussion following (1.14), and Remark~8.1}. The second-moment
result of this book does not identify either first-moment constant.

The positive-control variational problem provides a quantity to investigate.
Proposition~\ref{P:variational-positive-scores} and
Theorem~\ref{T:intrinsic-second-moment} give
\[
  S_1^{\mathrm{pos}}=2^{-m}C_2,
  \qquad m=\frac{4-\alpha}{3-\alpha}.
\]
This identity concerns control suprema. It does not prove that either
$t^{-\kappa}\log\E u(t,x)$ or $t^{-\kappa}\log\E|u(t,x)|$ converges to
$S_1^{\mathrm{pos}}$. Before proposing such an identification, we must check it
against the actual mean bounds, the static limit and the source's covariance
normalization. A variational characterization of the first mean may require
a different functional.

The main missing lower-bound argument concerns cancellation. The second-moment
proof restricts a nonnegative squared observable to a favorable event. For a
signed observable, the contribution from the complementary event cannot be
discarded. Positivity of the unconditional Wick coefficients does not supply
this comparison. Absolute values remove that particular obstruction, but a
first absolute-moment argument still needs a comparison between the localized
observable and the original solution, together with a matching upper bound.
The completed quadratic comparisons do not provide these statements.

There is a useful boundary calibration. At $\alpha_0=0$, Chen--Hu's static
first-mean asymptotic is an imported result; see~\eqref{E:static-ratio-input}.
Corollary~\ref{C:static-moment-constant-continuity} shows that the signed
first-mean lower and upper growth rates approach that constant as
$\alpha_0\downarrow0$, after the large-time liminf and limsup have been taken.
It does not establish an exact time limit at a fixed positive $\alpha_0$.
A first step is to test a proposed first-moment formula on the small-order
pairing coefficients and this static calibration, then isolate the needed
cancellation or localization estimate. We leave the existence and variational
identification of both first-moment limits open.
