\paragraph{Compactness of optimizing controls.}
The variational formula raises a question about the controls responsible for
large moment growth. Its admissible tests are smooth and compactly supported,
so attainment in that class should not be presumed. We first ask for a weaker
form of compactness in the one-dimensional spatial-white case.

Fix $\Gamma = c\delta_0$, with $c > 0$, and write $a = \alpha_0 \in (0,1)$.
Let $H_a$ be the completion of real smooth compactly supported tests on
$I\times\R$, where $I = (0,1)$, modulo covariance-null tests, under the norm
\[
  \|\Phi\|_{H_a}^2
  =c\int_{I^2}|s-t|^{-a}\int_\R
  \Phi(s,x)\Phi(t,x)\ud x\ud s\ud t.
\]
For $\Psi \in \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{pos}}$
of~\eqref{E:intro-control-class}, put
\[
  J_a(\Psi)=2\int_I\sqrt{\mathcal{R}_\Psi(s)}\ud s
  -\frac12\|\Psi\|_{H_a}^2,
  \qquad (\tau_y\Psi)(s,x)=\Psi(s,x+y).
\]
The Rayleigh value is defined in~\eqref{E:intro-control-rate}.

\begin{conjecture}[Compactness of an optimizing sequence]
  \label{Conj:positive-control-compactness}
  For every $c > 0$ and $0 < a < 1$, there are
  $\Psi_j \in \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{pos}}$, spatial translations $y_j \in \R$
  and a nonzero $\Phi_* \in H_a$ such that
  \[
    J_a(\Psi_j)\longrightarrow C_2(c\delta_0,a),
    \qquad
    \|\tau_{y_j}\Psi_j-\Phi_*\|_{H_a}\longrightarrow0.
  \]
\end{conjecture}

The conjecture asks for one strongly convergent optimizing sequence after
spatial translation. It asserts neither compactness of every optimizing
sequence nor a smooth compactly supported maximizer. Identifying the Rayleigh
functional at the limit would be a further step. Another question is whether
controls that separate into a time factor and a space factor give the full
supremum. Comparing such controls with sums of two products is a first test;
we do not assume either equality or strict improvement.

\paragraph{A sharper bound near the static case.}
The lower and upper formulas in Theorem~\ref{T:white-moment-bounds} have the
same limit as $a\downarrow0$. A more precise question concerns the first
correction to this limit.

\begin{conjecture}[First correction in the spatial-white case]
  \label{Conj:white-first-correction}
  For each fixed $c > 0$,
  \[
    \lim_{a\downarrow0}\frac1a
    \left(\frac{3C_2(c\delta_0,a)}{2\sqrt c}-1\right)
    =\frac34.
  \]
\end{conjecture}

The coefficient is suggested by formally expanding a temporally constant
trial and the temporal covariance operator around the constant kernel.
Justifying the trial approximation and a matching upper estimate remains part
of the question. The common zeroth-order limit of the existing bounds does
not establish this first-order correction.

For a fixed $0 < a < 1$, another question is whether both inequalities in
Theorem~\ref{T:white-moment-bounds} are strict. Varying the temporal equilibrium
trial may improve the lower formula. On the upper side, the norm of the
temporal covariance operator may improve the current row-integral estimate.
A separate numerical objective is a certified enclosure of $C_2$ for a fixed
$a$, with explicit errors from truncation and discretization. Optimizing a
finite family of controls alone gives lower bounds and does not certify the
continuum supremum from above.
