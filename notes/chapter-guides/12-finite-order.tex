A proposed asymptotic argument should first survive the smallest nontrivial
terms of the expansion. In the Stratonovich model these terms already contain
self-pairings, cross-pairings and products of different orders. Explicit
calculations make their multiplicities and normalization visible before any
large-order estimate is attempted.

This appendix uses the mixed expansion in Proposition~\ref{P:mixed} and works in
one spatial dimension with initial data $(1,0)$. A total order is the sum of the
two Stratonovich orders; it is not a Wiener-chaos order. We first compute all
total-order-four contributions for spatial white noise. We then give the
order-two coefficient and an order-four integral for Riesz covariance. The final
comparison records what these formulas test about scaling, pairing counts and
the static temporal limit. They provide checks on later estimates, but finitely
many coefficients do not determine the large-time exponent or its limiting
constant. Readers interested primarily in concrete examples can begin here after
consulting the chain notation in Chapter~\ref{S:G0}.
