Finite propagation makes spatial localization useful, but the required region
grows with the observation time. A bound whose constant is uncontrolled in the
localization radius cannot therefore be inserted into the long-time asymptotics.
This chapter separates estimates that are uniform in that radius from stochastic
estimates whose radius dependence must be displayed.

We use the covariance and phase constructions of
Chapters~\ref{S:book-gaussian-foundations}--\ref{S:book-wave-evolution} and the
mean maps of Chapter~\ref{S:book-means}. Section~\ref{S:uniform-mean-regulation}
gives uniform mean and Cameron bounds on the displacement--velocity Sobolev
phase; it does not claim such bounds for the additional constant coordinates.
Section~\ref{S:radius-stochastic-anchor} tracks the radius in Gaussian path
moments and estimates averages over time blocks.
Section~\ref{S:signed-static-wave} constructs the physical wave for completed
static mean potentials, which may have either sign. This last step allows the
upper argument to retain its full Gaussian control class. Positivity will enter
later through a separate variational comparison.
