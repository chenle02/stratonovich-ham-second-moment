Finite propagation makes spatial localization useful, but the region we need
grows with the observation time (Figure~\ref{Fig:light-cone}, in the next
chapter). A bound with an uncontrolled constant in the radius is useless for
long time. This chapter sorts the estimates into two kinds: those uniform
in the radius, and stochastic ones that must display it.

Section~\ref{S:uniform-mean-regulation} gives uniform mean and Cameron bounds
on the displacement--velocity Sobolev phase; it claims nothing of the kind for
the additional constant coordinates. Section~\ref{S:radius-stochastic-anchor}
tracks the radius in the Gaussian path moments and estimates averages over
time blocks. Section~\ref{S:signed-static-wave} builds the physical wave
for completed static mean potentials of either sign, so the upper argument
keeps all its Gaussian controls.

We use the covariance and phase constructions of
Chapters~\ref{S:book-gaussian-foundations}--\ref{S:book-wave-evolution}
and the mean maps of Chapter~\ref{S:book-means}. On a first reading, keep
Propositions~\ref{P:uniform-mean-regulator} and~\ref{P:signed-physical-wave},
and let the proofs wait. The restriction to nonnegative controls enters later,
through a separate variational comparison. Episode~\VideoRef{7}{Z35qRxt05kA}
of the video series covers this chapter and the next.
