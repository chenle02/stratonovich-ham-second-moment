The upper bound must account for all Gaussian fluctuations, not only the
favorable shifts used for the lower bound. It must also control the moment at
one spatial point while the propagation region grows. We address these two
problems together, keeping the original covariance norm in the Gaussian cost.

The prerequisites are the mean estimates of Chapter~\ref{S:book-means}, the
radius bounds of Chapter~\ref{S:book-growing-radius}, and the exact scaling in
Section~\ref{S:exact-canonical-scaling}. We first establish the symplectic and
norm properties of the wave evolution. Gaussian enlargement then reduces a
probabilistic estimate to growth under deterministic mean controls. Uniform
spectral and product estimates make this reduction compatible with the growing
radius. Section~\ref{S:original-point-transfer} returns to the original point
moment through finite propagation and an average of squares. Finally,
Section~\ref{S:original-signed-score-upper} controls the Gaussian tail and
obtains an upper variational expression over signed controls. Matching this
expression to the nonnegative-control lower value is the task of the next
chapter.
