To bound a moment from below, push the Gaussian noise toward a deterministic
potential that makes the wave grow, and pay for the push. The noise is
correlated in time, so the cost of one control is charged once, in the
original covariance space. It is not a sum over independent time blocks
(Figure~\ref{Fig:global-control-blocks}).

Section~\ref{S:exact-canonical-scaling} turns long time into small
noise by an equality in law. Section~\ref{S:actual-raw-block-lower}
proves, block by block in time, a pointwise lower bound for each
nonnegative smooth control (Proposition~\ref{P:raw-block-original-lower}).
Section~\ref{S:unmasked-variational} removes the spatial mask and optimizes
over controls, including those that do not factor into time and space
(Figure~\ref{Fig:nonseparable-controls}).
