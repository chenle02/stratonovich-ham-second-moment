Can estimates for simpler covariances be transferred to a more general
homogeneous covariance? Such a comparison requires more than a pointwise
factorization: the proposed factors must themselves be valid covariances, and
the regularized comparison must respect their amplitudes and scaling. These
requirements become especially important for measure-valued and anisotropic
covariances.

We retain Hypothesis~\ref{H:homog} and use the mixed pairing formula of
Chapter~\ref{S:G0}. Section~\ref{S:planar-holder} constructs planar endpoints
and proves the corresponding transfer under its stated hypotheses.
Section~\ref{S:three-endpoints} gives three-dimensional product constructions
and a conditional transfer; it does not assert a factorization for every
three-dimensional covariance. Finally, Section~\ref{S:energy-fidelity}
identifies the physical and spectral forms of the spatial energy for measure
covariances. This last identity does not remove the assumptions required by the
moment transfers. The appendix provides alternative comparisons and
normalization checks. The independent upper bound and the intrinsic constant in
the main chapters do not rely on these factorizations.
