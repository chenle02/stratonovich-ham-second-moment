Which solution do we mean, and which terms does its second moment contain? A
Stratonovich integral keeps the pairings inside one ordered chain as well as
the pairings between two chains (Figure~\ref{Fig:two-chain-pairings}). So
products of different orders need not have zero expectation, and dropping
them would change the moment under study.

We work with the model of Section~\ref{S:model}. Proposition~\ref{P:mixed}
gives the complete formula for the mixed
moments. Lemma~\ref{L:joint-fixed-order} constructs each fixed-order term. The
summable bounds of Lemma~\ref{L:vertex-crude} then construct their series, and
Proposition~\ref{P:mild-construction} identifies it as a mild solution. These
results give the construction and mixed-moment formula used later.
