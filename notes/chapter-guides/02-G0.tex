Before estimating the second moment, we must specify the solution and the terms
that contribute to it. Stratonovich integrals retain pairings within one ordered
chain as well as pairings between two chains. Consequently, products of
different orders need not have zero expectation. Omitting these terms would
change the moment under study.

We work with the model and covariance of Section~\ref{S:model}. Starting from
the ordered expansion in Section~\ref{S:known}, we derive the complete mixed
moment formula in Proposition~\ref{P:mixed}. We then examine a low-order term
and the bounds that lead to the coarse quadratic-exponential estimate. Finite
product estimates and joint limits construct the series, and
Proposition~\ref{P:mild-construction} verifies its mild equation. The final
section determines what homogeneity recovers from a common bound for each
pairing. That bound still loses the target growth scale. This chapter therefore
provides the solution used later and identifies a limitation of one estimation
method; the sharper upper argument begins in
Chapter~\ref{S:book-independent-upper}.
