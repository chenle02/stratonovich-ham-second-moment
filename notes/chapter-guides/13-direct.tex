The full upper bound in Chapter~\ref{S:book-independent-upper} covers all the
standing covariances. In simpler cases, direct estimates of the pairing
integrals give a shorter route and more explicit constants. This appendix
records those estimates and identifies the extra assumptions needed when the
spatial covariance is more singular.

The starting point is the mixed expansion in Proposition~\ref{P:mixed}.
Section~\ref{S:d1-upper} estimates spatial factors before summing the temporal
pairings. It reaches the correct scale for spatial homogeneity exponents at most
one, with separate one-dimensional Riesz and white-noise constants.
Section~\ref{S:higher-alpha} treats larger exponents under an additional
translated convolution bound and records obstructions to that assumption.
Section~\ref{S:static-holder} gives another route for isotropic Riesz
covariances by interpolation with static noise. The restrictions in these
sections are part of their statements. These arguments neither replace the
full-class proof nor identify the sharp moment constant, but they show where
simpler estimates suffice and where they lose information.
