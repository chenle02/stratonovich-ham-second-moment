Theorem~\ref{T:full-upper} covers all of Hypothesis~\ref{H:homog}, and its
proof is long. Is there a shorter way in simpler cases? Yes: estimate the
pairing integrals of Proposition~\ref{P:mixed} directly. The price is extra
assumptions; the reward, explicit constants. This appendix has no figure.

Section~\ref{S:d1-upper} bounds the spatial factors first
and then sums the temporal pairings. It reaches the correct
scale when $\alpha\le 1$ (Proposition~\ref{P:low-alpha-upper}),
with separate constants for one-dimensional Riesz and white noise
(Proposition~\ref{P:d1-upper}). Section~\ref{S:higher-alpha} goes beyond
$\alpha=1$, but only under an extra bound on translated convolutions
(Proposition~\ref{P:higher-transfer}); Proposition~\ref{P:higher-obstructions}
shows where that bound fails. Section~\ref{S:static-holder} gives a third
route for isotropic Riesz covariance, by interpolation with static noise.

Each restriction is part of its statement. These bounds neither replace
the proof of Chapter~\ref{S:book-independent-upper} nor give the sharp
constant, and the main argument does not rely on them. On a first reading,
Section~\ref{S:d1-upper} is enough. Episode~\VideoRef{9}{eLz9Hd20ZT0} of
the video series covers this appendix.
