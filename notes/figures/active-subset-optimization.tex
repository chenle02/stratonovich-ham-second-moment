\caption[Minimize within each subset, then maximize over subsets]{The finite-subset identity includes the empty set with value zero.
For each nonempty subset the inner problem is a strictly convex minimization
in positive weights, followed by a maximum over subsets. This does not assert
global concavity of the original objective, uniqueness of its maximizer,
or an effective algorithm. No inverse Gram matrix is used, so coincident
vectors and singular Gram matrices remain allowed. See
\ProofRef{Lemma}{L:gram-dual-minimum} and
\ProofRef{Proposition}{P:gram-positive-optimizer}.}
