# No-go counts for Appendix E

`nogo-counts.json` is the first snapshot, of November 10, 2026: the 42
failed approaches and corrections recorded then, by id, kind and status only.
It is kept unchanged. Appendix E counts the dated snapshot named in
`../selection.json`, at present `nogo-counts-2026-11-16.json` with 44 records
(the same records as `nogo-counts-2026-11-15.json`, re-affirmed without
change), and quotes the first snapshot only where the text says so.

The full ledger (each record's route, the reason it fails, its scope and
where it is recorded) is kept in the project's private files and is not
published here. Some records discuss possible errors in the unpublished
author version of the source paper, which is held back until its author has
been told, and some refer to a collaborators' unpublished project. The main
mathematical no-gos are stated in the book itself, with their proofs or
scope remarks; Appendix E points to them.
