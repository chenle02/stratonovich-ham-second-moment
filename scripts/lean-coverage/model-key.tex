% Key to the registered model choices M1-M6 (from the companion's model-choice register; one sentence each).
\item[M1] In dimension three the wave Green measure is defined through Mathlib's sphere measure; that it equals the surface measure used in the text is not proved in Lean.
\item[M2] The Gaussian space over finitely many coordinates is represented by normalized Hermite coefficient vectors; that Bochner integrals in it agree with the coordinatewise integrals is not proved in Lean.
\item[M3] The smoothed covariance $p_\varepsilon*\Gamma$ is taken as the measure with density $\gamma_\varepsilon$; that it equals Mathlib's convolution of measures is not proved in Lean.
\item[M4] The two-chain diagram integrals are the deterministic integrals of a diagram model; their identification with the joint limit in the text at arbitrary vertexwise widths is not proved in Lean (the positions form is proved in dimension one).
\item[M5] The completion spaces $H_t\otimes G$, $H_t$, $H_I$ and $H_\Gamma$ are realized as $L^2$ completions under the kernel and covariance forms; no tensor-product identification and no isometry between $H_{\mathrm{sp}}$ and $H_\Gamma$ is proved in Lean.
\item[M6] $H^1$ is the graph of the weak gradient in $L^2\times L^2$, with the topology of Mathlib's $H^1$; that every real element of Mathlib's $H^1$ arises from it is not proved in Lean.
